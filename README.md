# Root-exponential-error-bound-for-Laguerre-Gauss-quadrature
We prove that the $n$-point Laguerre-Gauss quadrature converges at a root-exponential rate when the integrand is analytic inside and on a parabola with focus at the origin and opening to the right, and decays no slower than $\mathrm{e}^{-a\sqrt{x}} (a>0)$  at infinity.
