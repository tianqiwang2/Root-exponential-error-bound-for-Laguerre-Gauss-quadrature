# Root-exponential asymptotic error bound for Laguerre-Gauss quadrature
**Authors:** Sihong Shao, Tianqi Wang

## Abstract

The first proof of the root-exponential convergence of Laguerre-Gauss quadrature was claimed in [Math. Comp. 93 (2024) 2861]. However, the analysis there requires the asymptotic behavior to hold uniformly up to infinity, which was not properly justified, and has recently been regarded as incomplete [arXiv:2609.11259]. To remedy this incompleteness, we split the error into an asymptotic part and a truncation part with approximately the largest root of the Laguerre polynomials as the dividing point, thereby avoiding the use of uniform asymptotic behavior up to infinity. We prove that the $n$-point Laguerre-Gauss quadrature converges at a root-exponential rate when the integrand is analytic inside and on a parabola with focus at the origin and opening to the right, and decays no slower than $\mathrm{e}^{-a\sqrt{x}} (a>0)$  at infinity. Our analysis applies to a larger class of functions and permits a suitable scaling technique to improve the convergence rate. We investigate the asymptotic behavior of Laguerre polynomials and Laguerre functions of the second kind in the complex plane. We also present some properties of Laguerre functions of the second kind that are crucial to our analysis.
